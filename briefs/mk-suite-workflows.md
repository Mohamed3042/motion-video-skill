## Direction

Every scene is a miniature workflow with an input, visible control/action and result. Product title occupies a small header; the interface and changing content are the hero. Distinct materials and composition for each product. Clearly label sample content DEMO. No screenshots of private data, no invented speed/accuracy/performance figures, no fake completed transactions. This is illustrated UI, not a running-app recording.

## Motion

Input visible at frame0, setup readable by24. Main action at60. Result by96or120, held to cut. For4.8s scenes a second action can happen at120withhold toend. Keep the existing150BPM score and53synchronized impacts. Twelve-frame designed wipes between worlds. Eased pointer arcs, click ripples, connected transforms, selected-state feedback, sliding panels, masked text reveals, waveform/track/dieline transformations. No meaningless continuous spinning.
